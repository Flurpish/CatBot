[Admin+]
/addbuttons {messageID} - Adds weekly buttons to a CatBot message.
/adminadd {user} - Adds a user to admin.
/adminremove {user} - Removes a user from admin.
/botadminadd {user} - Adds a user to bot admin (Admin+ only).
/botadminremove {user} - Removes a user from bot admin (Admin+ only).
/clearbuttons {messageID} - Removes all buttons from a message.
/resetweek - Resets all hours for every user.
/sendembed {title} {description} - Sends an embedded message.
/adddaily {day} - Adds a button for the desired day to the preset message.

[Admin]
/add {day} {game} {hours} {user to add to} - Adds hours to a user.
/remove {day} {game} {hours} {user to remove from} - Removes hours from a user.
/report - Provides a spreadsheet of all current hours.
/reset {user} {optional: day} - Resets all hours for a user. Adding a day will reset that day only.
/hours {user} - Shows a user's hours. Admins can view other users' hours.

[Normal]
/adminlist - View the current bot admins and normal admins.
/commands - Shows a list of all commands.
/games - Shows the list of games.
/hours - Shows your logged hours for the week.
/ping - Shows the bot's latency.
